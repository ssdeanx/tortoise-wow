# The Real In-Game Macros (Works 100% of the Time)

In Vanilla WoW 1.12 / Turtle, raid markers are placed on your current target using these standard client macro commands (put them in /macro and drag them to your action bar):

Skull:
text
/script SetRaidTarget("target", 8)
X (Cross):
text
/script SetRaidTarget("target", 7)
Moon:
text
/script SetRaidTarget("target", 5)
Clear Mark:
text
/script SetRaidTarget("target", 0)
(You must be the Group / Raid Leader or an Assistant).

## Built-in .raid Script Commands

On your currently running server, the .raid Eluna script only provides:

Skull: .raid skull (or .raid focus)
Moon (CC): .raid moon (or .raid ccmark)
Clear Marks: .raid clearmarks
(There is no native .raid x or .raid cross on the currently running version).

## Telling the Bots What to Focus or CC (/p or /ra)

Once a target has an icon on it, you tell the bots which icon to hit using the Playerbot /p (party) or /ra (raid) commands:

- Focus Skull:

text
/ra rti skull
/ra attack rti

- Focus X (Cross):

text
/ra rti cross
/ra attack rti

- Crowd-Control Moon (Mage sheep, Warlock banish/fear, Rogue sap, Priest shackle, Hunter trap):

text
/ra rti cc moon

## Want the Tanks to Auto-Mark for You?

You can also let the tank bot automatically place icons on mobs as they pull:

text
/p co +mark rti

Here are the most useful quick commands and 1-line macros for AoE control, Healers focusing tanks, Tank swaps / taunts, and emergency combat management:

1. AoE vs. Single-Target Burst
Action Chat / Macro Command What It Does

- Enable AoE

.raid aoe -- (or /ra co +aoe) Turns ON Blizzard, Rain of Fire, Volley, Whirlwind, and Consecration for heavy trash packs.

- Disable AoE (Single Target)

.raid noaoe -- (or .raid single / /ra co -aoe) Forces all bots to strictly use single-target abilities. Crucial on CC packs so sheep/shackles/traps don't break.

## Healers Focusing the Tank & Pre-Healing

Action Chat / Macro Command What It Does

- Lock Healer to Tank

/w <HealerName> focus heal <TankName> -- Locks that specific healer onto the chosen tank. They will prioritize that tank over all other raid members.

- Pre-Healing Mode

/ra co +preheal -- Tells healers to calculate incoming boss melee swings and pre-cast big heals (Greater Heal, Healing Touch) before damage lands.

- Mass Decurse & Dispel

/ra co +cure,+cleanse  -- Forces Priests, Paladins, Druids, and Shamans to prioritize dispelling Magic, Poisons, Curses, and Diseases.

## Tank Swaps & Threat Management

Note: For major raid bosses (Ebonroc Shadow, Razorgore Conflagration, Vaelastrasz, Gluth Mortal Wound, Daelus Sunder Armor, Twin Emperors), the bot AI and tactics engine automatically swap tanks when debuff thresholds hit.

When you need to manually force a tank swap or pull aggro:

Action Chat / Macro Command What It Does

- Force Off-Tank to Taunt

/w <OfftankName> -- cast taunt(or cast growl for Bear Druid) Forces your off-tank bot to immediately taunt your current target.

- Main Tank Charge & Pull

/p tank attack -- (or /ra tank attack) Orders the main tank bot to immediately charge/shoot the target and establish initial threat.

- Controlled Corner Pull

/p pull -- followed by

/p follow -- Orders a ranged bot to shoot the target and immediately sprint back around a corner for line-of-sight pulls.

## Emergency & Utility Commands

Action Chat / Macro Command What It Does

- Emergency Flee (Disengage)

/ra flee -- Cancels all bot casting and orders every bot to sprint directly to your location immediately.

- Hold Position (Freeze)
/ra stay -- Locks bots in place (useful when setting up pulls or avoiding patrol pats).

- Resume Follow
/ra follow -- Releases the stay lock and resumes following the master.

- Vacuum Loot & Drink Break
.raid rest -- (or /ra food, /ra drink) Commands all injured bots to eat and all casters to drink back to 100% mana.

- Vacuum Loot
.raid loot -- (or /ra loot) Directs bots to loot all nearby slain corpses.
